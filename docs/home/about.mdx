Syllables develops tools for working with lyrics and researches how Apple Music represents and displays them. The work spans preparing lyric files, parsing their contents, and rendering them in music players.

## Projects

[Vault](../vault/index.mdx) is a TTML lyrics contribution platform, centered on community contributions of lyric files.

Syllables also develops tools and libraries for lyrics and music applications. The [Packages](../packages/index.mdx) section brings their documentation together.

## Research and direction

Alongside the tools, Syllables investigates Apple Music APIs and lyric formats. The wider plans include a shared lyrics standard and integrations with other music platforms.

The [project website](https://syllables.dev/about) describes this direction in more detail.
