## Words and punctuation

Use one space between words. Put a space after a comma, question mark, or exclamation point when another word follows. Do not put a space before those marks or immediately inside parentheses and quotation marks.

### Use a plain space, not a look-alike

A non-breaking space (U+00A0) and an ideographic space (U+3000) look like a space and are not one. A zero-width space (U+200B) is invisible in every editor and still ends up in the file. All three survive a copy and paste out of a word processor or a lyric website.

| Character | Name | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `U+0020` | Ordinary space | Use this one |
| `U+00A0` | No-break space | Replace with an ordinary space |
| `U+3000` | Ideographic space | Replace with an ordinary space |
| `U+200B` | Zero-width space | Remove |

Check for them before delivery. A line that looks right and sorts, searches, or splits wrongly usually contains one.
