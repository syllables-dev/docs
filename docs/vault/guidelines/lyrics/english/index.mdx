title: English
description: Guidelines for English lyrics
---

Write lyrics that match the recording and are easy to follow. Use consistent spelling, capitalization, and punctuation, and let the vocal phrasing guide the line breaks.

## Keep the artist's words

Preserve slang, contractions, repetition, and intentional nonstandard grammar. Correct spelling and transcription mistakes without rewriting the lyric into formal English.

Use the exact version of the recording you are transcribing. A live performance, clean edit, or alternate recording may have different words.

Apple Music transcriptions can contain mistakes. Trust your ears, and cross-reference different sources whenever possible.

## Make the text readable

- Begin each lyric line with a capital letter and use normal capitalization within the line.
- Use punctuation to clarify meaning. Do not add a period or comma to finish a line.
- Break lines at natural vocal phrases. A sentence may continue across several lines.
- Put each verse, chorus, and other distinct part of the song in its own section.
- Write repeated lyrics in full each time they are performed.
- Read each line together with the lines around it. Questions, quotations, and other thoughts can cross line boundaries.
